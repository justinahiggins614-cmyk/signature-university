? Guide — how to use this site

Everything here is free, and your progress stays on your device — no account, no tracking.

📚 Course Catalog

Search all 3,850 courses by name, code, or course ID. Filter by college and level, or browse A–Z. The Course Finder takes plain words — try “welding”.

🎓 Courses

Tap any course card to open its full record: Overview, Syllabus (6 modules, 24 lessons), Readings (cross-linked to the dictionary, wiki, live specs and patents, books), Teacher, and Labs tabs. Every course has a shareable deep link like ?course=JAH-COURSE-000260 — it survives refresh.

🤖 AI teacher & campus guide

Every course has its own AI teacher: type “syllabus”, “quiz”, or “help”. The AI Guide Chat wing maps your goals (“I want to become an electrician”) to real courses and degree paths. Quick answers on any course overview come from that course’s own record.

🧪 Teaching Laboratories

11,550 hands-on lab exercises from every course. Search them, tap one to open its course.

🏗 Capstone Projects · 🔬 Research Wing · 📖 Library

Capstone builds per college; the Research Wing shows how new specs and patents become course material; the Library shelves dictionary and wiki words per college.

🎓 Degrees & Diplomas

Pick a degree, finish every item in its material list, then pass the 10-question official test (official within the Signature system, 70% to pass, retakes allowed). Your degree is awarded, stamped, and sealed — print the diploma, download the degree record, or verify it at Verify a diploma. The university claims no government accreditation; diplomas certify course completion.

💾 Your progress

Completed courses, degrees, and test attempts save automatically in this browser. Set your diploma name, export a JSON backup, import it on another device, or download a transcript (JSON / CSV / TXT).

🔊 Read aloud · ◐ Theme · ⚠ Report

The floating 🔊 button reads any page aloud. The ◐ button toggles dark mode. “Report a problem” copies a ready-made report for the site owner.
