Verify a Diploma NOT GOVERNMENT ACCREDITED

A The Signature University diploma is a Certificate of Completion — it certifies completion of The Signature University coursework. Verification checks that a diploma record is intact and well-formed: its ID, its content hash, its degree, and its curriculum version. It does not verify the identity of the named learner — the name on a diploma is user-supplied.

Paste the diploma/degree record JSON — from the Download degree record button on your degree page. The hash is recomputed from this record.


What VALID means: the record's diploma ID matches its content (learner name + degree + award date), the SHA-256 content hash matches, the degree exists in the university catalog, and the curriculum version is current. What it does not mean: government accreditation, or independent confirmation of who completed the work.
